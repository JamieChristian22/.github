## Weekly measurement

Record actual GitHub traffic (views, unique visitors and clones where available), meaningful issues or contributions, stars, referral sources where available, LinkedIn post impressions and relevant conversations, and actual sponsorships. Use consistent weekly windows; leave unavailable values blank rather than assuming zero. GitHub traffic history may be limited, so record weekly snapshots. No baseline, conversion rate or growth outcome has yet been verified.

Success is useful discovery and genuine engagement. A sponsor button, sitemap, share link or post draft does not establish indexing, traffic, sponsorship revenue or hiring outcomes.

## Before sharing

- Verify the public page and latest CI status.
- Label simulated scenarios and untested deployments.
- Share no credentials, sensitive screenshots, private datasets or proprietary material.
- Follow community rules and respect opt-outs.
- Do not use engagement pods, fake accounts, bulk unsolicited outreach or misleading claims.

The drafts above are prepared for manual review and publishing; they have not been posted externally.
