## Why support this work?

I build and document public projects across business, technology, and analytics. My aim is to make problem-solving more tangible through source code, reproducible exercises, analytical examples, templates, and clear documentation. Cloud engineering is one part of this work, not the entire portfolio.

A sponsorship is an optional way to support continued maintenance, testing, documentation, and new public resources. Every amount helps me allocate more time to making projects useful and easier to understand. Public access to existing repositories does not depend on sponsorship.

Sponsorship does not grant control over research conclusions, security findings, project priorities, or guaranteed custom work. Public materials remain available subject to each repository's terms.
