## One-week low-pressure rollout

- Day 1: Publish the launch post on LinkedIn. Share the landing page as the primary call to action.
- Day 2: Respond thoughtfully to comments. Share an actual repository with anyone whose question matches the work.
- Day 3: Publish Post 2, focusing on useful artifacts and actionable resources.
- Day 4: Participate helpfully in relevant developer or analyst discussions where sharing your work is permitted; avoid unsolicited links.
- Day 5 or 6: Publish Post 3, explaining what ongoing support makes possible.
- Day 7: Review each post's impressions, engagement, and link interactions. Compare with GitHub profile activity and Sponsors dashboard; improve the next week's message.

## Metrics and decisions

Record weekly: posts published, impressions, profile visits, clicks or link interactions when available, repository stars and substantive feedback, sponsor count, recurring sponsorships, one-time supporters, and opt-in supporter feedback.

Define a baseline before promising results; sponsored revenue and conversions may remain zero during early testing. Optimize for relevant engagement, genuine project usage, and sustainable support, not manufactured trends.

## Distribution rules

- Do not buy followers, fake engagement, or post repeated appeals in unrelated communities.
- Do not mass-DM requests for money.
- Keep sponsorship voluntary and distinguish educational simulations from deployed production systems.
- Publish a specific useful project between fundraising posts; repeat only when the information materially changes.
- Verify that social accounts are connected and posts are actually published; a draft or scheduled post isn't proof of distribution.

## Sponsorship disclosure

Support does not purchase guaranteed features, preferential research findings, endorsements, paid consulting, or special access to existing public resources. Honor the documented terms of each repository.
