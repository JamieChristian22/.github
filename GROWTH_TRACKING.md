## Four-week activity targets (not outcome promises)

- Publish one useful, evidence-based technical resource or post per week.
- Make one substantive community contribution or response per week where permitted.
- Inspect flagship CI results and address regressions.
- Review inbound inquiries and respond individually; no unsolicited bulk messages.
- Review actual traffic and improve the topics and resources that prove useful.
